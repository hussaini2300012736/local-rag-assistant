Formula 1 is the highest class of international single-seater auto racing, organized by the FIA. Each season consists of a series of races called Grands Prix, held on a mix of purpose-built circuits and closed public roads across different countries. Points are awarded to the top ten finishers in each race, and these points accumulate over the season to decide two championships: the Drivers' Championship and the Constructors' Championship.

A typical race weekend includes practice sessions, qualifying, and the race itself. Qualifying determines the starting grid order for the race, with the fastest driver starting from pole position. Some weekends also include a shorter sprint race that awards its own smaller points.
