The financial rules changed alongside the technical ones. For 2026, the FIA raised the team operational cost cap from $135 million to $215 million, largely to account for inflation and to fold in some costs that were previously excluded from the cap. Driver salaries, the salaries of a team's three highest-paid staff, marketing, travel, hospitality, and infrastructure investment all remain outside the cap. A separate cost cap specifically for power unit manufacturers also rose, from $95 million to $130 million, to help fund development of the new hybrid power units.

On the sporting side, the mandatory minimum two pit stops at the Monaco Grand Prix, introduced in 2025 to encourage more action, was dropped again for 2026 after teams and drivers concluded it hadn't meaningfully improved the racing. The 2026 calendar runs to 24 Grands Prix, starting with the Australian Grand Prix in March, and includes a new race in Madrid replacing the Italian Grand Prix at Imola.
