Constructors, commonly called teams, design and build their own cars each season within a strict technical rulebook set by the FIA. Since 2021, teams have operated under a cost cap, limiting how much they can spend on car development in a season, in an effort to keep competition closer between wealthier and smaller teams.

Each team fields two drivers per season. Drivers earn points individually for the Drivers' Championship, while their combined points contribute to their team's total in the Constructors' Championship. The Constructors' Championship carries significant prize money, which is why team strategy sometimes prioritizes securing points for both cars rather than favoring a single driver.
