The 2026 grid expanded to eleven teams for the first time since 2016. Audi joined as a full factory team, having acquired the Sauber team in 2024 and run it under the Kick Sauber name through 2025 before switching to Audi branding and its own power unit for 2026; the team kept its 2025 drivers, Nico Hulkenberg and Gabriel Bortoleto. Cadillac became an entirely new eleventh team, entering with veteran drivers Sergio Perez and Valtteri Bottas and using Ferrari power units.

Engine supply also shifted: Honda returned as a fully independent power unit supplier for the first time since 2021, now supplying Aston Martin rather than the Red Bull-owned teams. Red Bull, in turn, ended its relationship with Honda and launched its own in-house power unit under Red Bull Powertrains, done in partnership with Ford, which returned to Formula 1 for the first time since 2004. Renault stopped supplying engines to Alpine from 2026, marking the first season without Renault as an engine supplier since 2000.
